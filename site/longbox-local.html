How the sale runs

  1. One issue at a time. A character is pulled from the box at random. Nobody knows what is coming next.
  2. Open outcry. Either buyer opens at $1 or more. The other raises or passes. A pass ends the lot and the standing bid takes the issue.
  3. Both pass, no sale. The issue goes back in the box and the next one comes up.
  4. Keep a dollar per slot. You can never bid so deep that you cannot afford $1 for each empty slot, so both teams always reach five.
  5. Unopposed run. Once one roster is full, the other buyer takes lots alone at $1 each. Digging past one costs $1 too, so spare cash buys you choices — until your money equals your empty slots and you take what comes.
  6. Five against five. Base versions, random encounter. Copy the rosters out at the end and get the fight written up.

Condition grades are cosmetic. A Near Mint Rhino is still a Rhino.

Best played on one shared screen over a video call — bids are public, so there is nothing to hide.
