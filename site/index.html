How the sale runs

  1. Twenty lots, ten sold. Twenty characters come out of the box one at a time, in a random order. You each buy five. The other ten pass in unsold.
  2. Open outcry. Bid against each other in dollars. When you stop bidding, hit Pass and the standing bid takes the lot at that price.
  3. Nobody bids, it passes in. If you both pass before anyone opens, that character is gone for good. That is how the other ten leave the box.
  4. Passes are finite, and you never count them. Ten lots have to be bought, so every lot you let go is one less chance to be choosy. The header shows what is left.
  5. Then it becomes compulsory. When the lots remaining equal the buys still owed, passing stops. Whatever comes up, somebody is buying it.
  6. Keep a dollar per slot. You can never bid so deep that you cannot afford $1 for each empty slot, so both rosters always reach five.
  7. Buying alone costs to walk away. Once the other side is full, each pass you make costs $1, so a fat purse buys patience and nothing else.
  8. Then they fight. Base versions. Pick a random encounter or a week of prep, and the issue gets fought on the spot.

Tiers are the only number on the table, and they decide the fight. Everything else is what you already know.

Two or three buyers, any devices, anywhere in the world. Every bid is public, so talk your way into a bargain.
