How the sale runs

  1. One issue at a time. A character is pulled from the box at random. Nobody knows what is coming next.
  2. Nothing goes back in the box. Every issue drawn ends up on a roster. Exactly ten come out, five each, and there is no passing or no-sale.
  3. Someone has to open. The obligation alternates each lot. The opener bids $1 or more; the other side raises, or says Yours and hands it over at the standing price.
  4. Yours is a weapon. Say it before anyone has bid and your opponent takes the issue for $1, whether they wanted it or not. A slot is a slot.
  5. Keep a dollar per slot. You can never bid so deep that you cannot afford $1 for each empty slot, so both teams always reach five.
  6. Unopposed. Once one roster is full, every remaining issue goes straight to the other buyer at $1.
  7. Five against five. Base versions, random encounter. Copy the rosters out at the end and get the fight written up.

Tiers are the only number on the table. Everything else is what you already know.

Two devices, anywhere in the world. Every bid is public, so keep a call open and talk your way into a bargain.
