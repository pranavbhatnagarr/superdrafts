How to read a row

  1. Printed. The tier on the card. Nobody is printed at S+, and no amount of climbing takes anyone past it.
  2. A fitting role. Put a character in a job that suits them and they fight one rung higher. Put them in one that does not and they drop a rung, which is the fall this guide does not print.
  3. A week of prep. Worth however many rungs their prep rating says, nought to two, and only in a prep encounter. A random encounter buys none of it.
  4. Ceiling. Both at once, which is the best that character can ever fight at. It is also the only road to S+.
