Privacy Policy - Super Drafts

Super Drafts is a small game built for friends. This page explains what happens when you sign in with Google.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture, as provided by Google. This is used only to identify you at the table you're playing at and to keep a simple win/loss record and any game preferences you choose to save.

Profile pictures

Once you have a profile, you can optionally upload your own picture to use in the game, replacing the one Google provided. An uploaded picture is stored and shown to other players at your table, the same way your name already is. You can remove it at any time, which returns your profile to using your Google account's picture by default.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, share, or otherwise disclose your information to anyone.
  • We don't use it for advertising or marketing.
  • We don't share it with any third party beyond the infrastructure that runs the game itself (Supabase, which stores game data).

Guest play

Signing in with Google is optional. You can play without an account by typing a name; no Google data is collected in that case.

Deleting your data

If you'd like your account and its data removed, contact the person who runs this game and ask them to delete your profile.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the person who invited you to play.
